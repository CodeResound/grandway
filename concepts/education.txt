# EDUCATION — Grandway

> **Status: concept only.** This module is not built. The concept is written now
> so the boundary between it and the applicant record is settled before either
> is implemented. Building it requires its own session and the §25/§26 gates.

## Purpose

The Education module owns an applicant's academic history — what they have
studied, where, when, and how well.

It exists as a separate module because academic history is a growing collection
with its own shape, while the applicant record is a single permanent identity.
One person accumulates a school-leaving certificate, a bachelor's degree, maybe
a diploma, maybe a gap year, maybe a training course. Flattening that onto the
applicant would either lose records or force an arbitrary limit.

## Relationship to the applicant

One applicant has many education records. Each record belongs to exactly one
applicant.

Education records are permanent facts about the person, not about any particular
study plan. A bachelor's degree does not belong to the Australia journey; it
belongs to the person, and every journey draws on it.

## Core entities

Education record

One completed or ongoing qualification. Should hold:

* Qualification level and title
* Institution attended and its country
* Awarding body, where it differs from the institution
* Start and end dates
* Whether it is completed or ongoing
* Grading — GPA, percentage, division, or grade, with the scale it is measured on
* Number of backlogs or failed papers
* Medium of instruction

Grading needs care. Nepali institutions report percentages, divisions, and GPAs
on different scales, and a foreign institution reading "3.2" needs to know
whether that is out of 4 or out of 5. The scale is part of the score, not
context to be remembered separately.

Education gap

A deliberate record of a period with no study. Gaps are a routine question in
admissions and visa assessment, so an unexplained gap is an operational problem
rather than merely missing data. Should hold the period, the reason, and any
supporting explanation.

Training

Short courses, certifications, and vocational training that are not full
qualifications but are worth recording.

## Permissions

Admin and Lead Manager may both create, view, edit, and remove education records
for any applicant, consistent with applicants and journeys being shared.

Whether an education record can be deleted outright or must be deactivated is an
open question — unlike leads and applicants, a wrongly entered qualification has
little historical value.

## Constraints / Out of scope

* No credential verification. Grandway records what the applicant reports and any
  supporting document reference; it does not confirm authenticity.
* No automatic eligibility assessment. Deciding whether a qualification meets an
  institution's entry requirement is not a Grandway decision.
* No grade conversion between systems. Storing the scale is in scope; converting
  a Nepali percentage to a foreign GPA is not.
* No transcripts or certificates as files — those belong to the uploaded files
  module, referenced from here.
* No employment or work-experience history; explicitly out of scope for V1 at
  the project level.

## Open questions

* Should qualification levels be a fixed enum or an Admin-configurable reference
  table, as lead sources are? Nepali qualification names vary and the initial set
  will not be complete.
* Is one grading field with a scale enough, or do GPA, percentage, and division
  need separate fields because an applicant may hold more than one form?
* Should education gaps be derived automatically from the dates of surrounding
  records, or always entered by hand? Deriving them is convenient but wrong when
  a record is simply missing.
* Should a record marked ongoing be required to have no end date, and should
  Grandway prompt when an expected end date passes?
* Can education records be deleted, or only deactivated?
* Does the applicant's highest qualification need to be a computed convenience
  field, and if so how is "highest" determined across dissimilar levels?
