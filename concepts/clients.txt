## Purpose

The Clients module keeps the consultancy's B2B client directory in one place.

It exists so Grandway can record the companies, agencies, schools, or partner
organizations that send or refer applicants, along with the basic contact and
identity details staff need to work with them consistently.

This is not a sales CRM. It is a compact reference directory for relationship
management and operational attribution.

## Relationship to other records

A client is a business organization, not an applicant and not a lead.

The client record stores the consultancy's external partner identity. Leads and
applicants remain person records. If a lead or applicant came through a client,
that relationship should be represented as a reference, not by copying the
client's details into the person record.

Clients are especially useful for tracking where referred leads originate,
which partner office handled a case, and which contact person should receive
updates.

## Core entities

Client

The business organization being tracked. It should hold the company or agency
name, current status, spokesperson or primary contact, contact numbers, email,
address, website, logo, and notes.

Client contact

The named person or people the consultancy communicates with at the client
organization. In V1 this may be represented directly on the client record as a
primary spokesperson and contact details rather than as a separate contact
table.

Availability / status

Whether the client is active, inactive, or otherwise not currently used. A
client should not be deleted just because the relationship paused or ended.

## Key user flows

1. Maintain client records: An Admin creates or updates a client -> records the
   company name, spokesperson, contact details, logo, email, address, website,
   and notes -> marks the client active or inactive as the relationship changes.
   - If the client is no longer used, it should be retired rather than deleted.
   - If a detail changes, the previous value should remain traceable through the
     normal audit history.

2. Find the right partner: A Lead Manager opens the clients directory -> looks
   up the company or contact person -> uses the record to confirm who to call or
   email for a referral, status update, or operational follow-up.
   - If the client is inactive, it should still be visible historically but not
     treated as a preferred current contact.

3. Attribution support: A lead or applicant originated from a client -> staff
   reference the client record when recording the source of the relationship ->
   reporting can later show which organizations are sending work.
   - The client module does not create or convert leads; it only supplies the
     reference record used by other workflows.

## UI screens & wireframe notes

Client List

Shows all client organizations in a searchable directory. Columns should show
company name, primary spokesperson, email or phone, status, and last updated
date. Primary action: add client.

Client Detail

Shows the full client record: company identity, contact information, logo,
website, address, notes, and status. Include a visible history or audit trail
panel if available. Primary actions: edit client, retire or restore client.

New / Edit Client Form

A straightforward form for maintaining the contact directory. Fields should be
kept intentionally small and practical: company name, spokesperson, contact
numbers, email, address, website, logo, notes, and status.

## Constraints / Out of scope

* No lead management. Clients may be referenced by leads, but client records do
  not replace lead records.
* No applicant management. A client is not a person profile and does not own
  applicant data.
* No pipeline, deal, or commission tracking.
* No task assignment or communication inbox.
* No document generation or contract workflow.
* No deletion of historical records. Inactive clients should be retained.
* No complex multi-contact CRM structure in V1 unless explicitly added later.

## Open questions

Settled in V1 (2026-07-24):

* **One spokesperson or multiple named contacts?** One, inline on the client
  record — name (Devanagari + English), designation, and the organization's
  email. Contact *numbers* are a child table, because an agency realistically
  has a landline and two mobiles while it has exactly one person you ask for.
  This honours both the concept's "may be represented directly on the client
  record" and its "no complex multi-contact CRM structure in V1".
* **Readable by both, writable by Admin only?** Yes — exactly as the question
  proposed, and exactly what flows 1 and 2 already describe. Every Admin and
  Lead Manager may read; only an Admin may create, edit, retire, or restore.
  Superadmin is denied outright. The directory is shared reference data: one
  careless edit to a spokesperson's number changes who every Lead Manager
  calls, so the write right is narrower than the read right. Same split as
  `institutions`, and only the second app in the project to use it.
* **Referral statistics in V1?** No, and not here. Attribution belongs to the
  `leads` app, at the moment a lead is created. **It is not built** — see the
  banner at the top of this file.
* **Logos as uploaded files or a field?** A `logo_url` link to a logo hosted
  elsewhere. A real upload needs a §14 file contract and would pre-empt the
  `uploaded_files` domain, which is exactly what `applicants` (photograph) and
  `offers` (supporting files) both declined to do. Replaceable additively when
  that module ships.
* **Is a status enum enough, or is a paused/archived distinction needed?** Two
  values are enough: `active` and `inactive`. A third status is only worth
  having if something branches on it, and nothing does — the only behaviour
  attached to status is whether the directory offers a partner as a current
  contact, which is binary. The nuance a "paused" value would have carried is
  carried better by the mandatory retirement reason, which says *why* in words.

Still open:

* When should `leads.Lead` gain a `client` reference, and should `applicants`
  get one too or inherit it through the lead?
* Should a client have a type (agency, school, partner company)? The concept's
  prose names all three but its field list omits a type, so V1 has none and
  they cannot be filtered apart.
* Should the directory warn about a probable duplicate on create? Two clients
  may currently share a name, email, and phone number with no warning.
* Is a bulk import needed for the initial directory, or is one-at-a-time entry
  acceptable?
* Should a retired client be excluded from pickers automatically, or is that a
  presentation choice left to each screen? The API returns both by default.
